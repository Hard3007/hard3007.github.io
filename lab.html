How it works

  1. Split the sentence into words. Each word is a token.
  2. Turn each word into a vector. This page builds vectors by hand: three-letter spelling chunks, sine-wave position codes from the original Transformer paper, and a small lexicon of about 80 engineering words.
  3. Score every pair of words. The score for word i looking at word j is the dot product of their vectors divided by the square root of the vector size.
  4. Optionally hide the future. With the causal mask on, scores for later words become negative infinity, so they get zero weight.
  5. Apply softmax. Each row of scores is divided by the temperature, then turned into weights that add up to 1.

This is a teaching toy, not a trained model. Real Transformers learn their vectors and projection matrices from huge amounts of text and run many heads across many layers. For the trained version, see my Transformer language model from scratch.
