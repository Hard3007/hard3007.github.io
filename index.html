About

I'm a machine learning engineer pursuing an MS in Computer Science at Northeastern University's Khoury College in Boston. My work sits where models meet real limits: satellite imagery that has to be processed on board, dense legal text that needs answers you can trace back to a clause, and detectors that have to keep up with a moving conveyor belt.

Before Northeastern I earned a B.Tech in Computer Engineering at NMIMS University and interned at ISRO's Space Applications Centre, SVNIT, Prowiz Infotech and Inficial Infotech. I also co-authored a peer-reviewed review of machine learning for waste management, published by Springer.
