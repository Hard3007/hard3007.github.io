# Demo video script (about 2 minutes)

Record the live site at https://hard3007.github.io/ in a browser window, with
your voice over the top. Upload it to YouTube as **Public** and put the link in
the README.

## 0:00 to 0:15 Introduction

> Hi, I'm Hard Gondaliya, an MS Computer Science student at Northeastern. This
> is my personal homepage for CS5610 Project 1, built with plain HTML, CSS and
> JavaScript modules, with no frameworks.

## 0:15 to 0:50 The interactive headline

Hover over "sees", then "reads", then "fits". Then press Tab a few times to
show keyboard focus moving across the words.

> The headline is my creative component. Every word is a button, and when I
> point at one, the arcs show which words it pays attention to, just like
> self-attention in a Transformer. "Sees" looks at "orbit", "reads" looks at
> "fine print", and "fits" looks at "chip". The weights are computed live in
> the browser with a softmax over dot products. It also works with the
> keyboard.

## 0:50 to 1:10 The rest of the homepage

Scroll slowly through About, Experience, Skills and Contact.

> Below that are my education, my internships at ISRO, SVNIT, Prowiz and
> Inficial, my skills, and my contact links. The layout uses a CSS grid with
> section titles on the left.

## 1:10 to 1:30 Projects page

Click Projects. Click "Computer vision", then "Systems and deployment". Point
at the URL bar.

> The projects page lists six projects and my Springer publication. These
> filter buttons narrow the list, and the filter is saved in the URL, so I can
> share a link straight to my deployment work.

## 1:30 to 1:55 Attention lab (AI-generated page)

Click Attention lab. Click the "FPGA chip" example, switch to "Position", turn
on the causal mask, then drag the temperature slider.

> The third page was generated with AI, and it's labelled that way. You can
> type any sentence and see a full attention heatmap. The causal mask hides
> future words like a GPT model, and temperature makes attention sharper or
> flatter.

## 1:55 to 2:05 Wrap up

Show the repository on GitHub, briefly.

> The code, design document and build instructions are in the GitHub
> repository. Thanks for watching.
